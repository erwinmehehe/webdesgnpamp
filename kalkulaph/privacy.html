KalkulaPH Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

KalkulaPH is a calculator-first mobile app developed by Erwin Valles for Philippine salary, tax, contribution, benefit and personal pay-planning estimates.

Information stored on your device

KalkulaPH may store saved calculation results and their inputs, an optional My Salary/My Pay profile, payday settings, job/employer history, money reminders, favorites, recently used calculators and display preferences locally on your device.

KalkulaPH does not require your name, email address, phone number, employer account or government ID to use its calculators and personal planning tools.

Local reminders and notifications

If you choose to enable a money reminder, KalkulaPH may request your device notification permission and schedule the reminder locally on that device. Reminder titles, dates and notes remain in local app/device storage. The current build does not obtain an Expo push token or send reminder content through a KalkulaPH remote push-notification backend.

Data collection and sharing

The current production implementation does not include advertising, analytics, cloud-sync, crash-reporting or user-account SDKs. KalkulaPH does not add your salary inputs, saved calculations, My Pay values, job history or reminders to its contribution-rate request.

Network use and contribution-rate refresh

Core calculations can run offline. KalkulaPH may automatically request a small public rate manifest hosted on GitHub Pages to check for reviewed SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG contribution-parameter updates. KalkulaPH does not add your salary, calculator inputs, job history, profile values or other user-entered financial information to that request.

Like a normal web request, GitHub Pages or related hosting infrastructure may receive technical request data such as an IP address, user agent and request time under its own privacy practices. Internet access is also used when you choose to open an external official-government source link, this privacy policy or the public support page.

Sharing

If you explicitly choose to share a result card or report an issue, your device's native sharing interface opens and you choose the destination. KalkulaPH does not automatically send the shared content anywhere.

External links

KalkulaPH links to official resources operated by agencies such as SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG Fund, BIR, DOLE and NWPC. Those websites have their own privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it. You can remove local data through More → Reset local data, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Resetting local data also clears KalkulaPH scheduled local reminders where the operating system permits it.

Children

KalkulaPH is a general salary and benefits utility and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Independent service

KalkulaPH is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official app of SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG Fund, BIR, DOLE or NWPC. Calculator outputs, benefit screens and planning scores are estimates.
