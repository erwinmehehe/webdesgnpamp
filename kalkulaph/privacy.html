KalkulaPH Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

KalkulaPH is a calculator-first mobile app developed by Erwin Valles for Philippine salary, tax, contribution and worker-benefit estimates.

Information stored on your device

KalkulaPH may store saved calculation results and their inputs, an optional My Salary profile, favorite and recently used calculators, and display preferences locally on your device.

KalkulaPH does not require your name, email address, phone number, employer account or government ID to use its core calculators.

Data collection and sharing

The current production build does not include advertising, analytics, cloud-sync, crash-reporting or user-account SDKs. KalkulaPH does not automatically transmit your calculator inputs, saved calculations or My Salary profile to the developer or third parties.

Network use

Core calculations can run offline. Internet access is used when you choose to open an external official-government source link or a public KalkulaPH support/privacy page.

Sharing

If you explicitly choose to share a result card or report an issue, your device's native sharing interface opens and you choose the destination. KalkulaPH does not automatically send the shared content anywhere.

External links

KalkulaPH links to official resources operated by agencies such as SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG Fund, BIR, DOLE and NWPC. Those websites have their own privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it. You can remove local data through the app's reset option where available, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

Children

KalkulaPH is a general salary and benefits utility and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Independent service

KalkulaPH is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official app of SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG Fund, BIR, DOLE or NWPC. Calculator outputs are estimates.
