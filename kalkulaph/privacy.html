KalkulaPH Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

KalkulaPH is a calculator-first mobile app developed by Erwin Valles for Philippine salary, tax, contribution, benefit and personal pay-planning estimates.

Information stored on your device

KalkulaPH may store saved calculation results and their inputs, an optional My Salary profile, optional My Pay planning values, job/employer history you enter, reminder preferences, favorite and recently used calculators, and display preferences locally on your device.

KalkulaPH does not require your name, email address, phone number, employer account or government ID to use its calculators and personal planning tools.

Data collection and sharing

The current production implementation does not include advertising, analytics, cloud-sync, crash-reporting or user-account SDKs. KalkulaPH does not automatically transmit your salary inputs, saved calculations, My Salary/My Pay values, job history or reminder settings to the developer or third parties.

Network use and contribution-rate refresh

Core calculations can run offline. KalkulaPH may automatically request a small public rate manifest hosted on this site to check for reviewed SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG contribution-parameter updates. The request does not contain your salary, calculator inputs, job history, profile values or other user-entered financial information.

Internet access is also used when you choose to open an external official-government source link, this privacy policy or the public support page.

Sharing

If you explicitly choose to share a result card or report an issue, your device's native sharing interface opens and you choose the destination. KalkulaPH does not automatically send the shared content anywhere.

External links

KalkulaPH links to official resources operated by agencies such as SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG Fund, BIR, DOLE and NWPC. Those websites have their own privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it. You can remove local data through Settings → Reset local data, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

Children

KalkulaPH is a general salary and benefits utility and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Independent service

KalkulaPH is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official app of SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG Fund, BIR, DOLE or NWPC. Calculator outputs and planning scores are estimates.
